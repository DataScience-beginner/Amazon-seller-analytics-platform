Freshness decisions:

- Current Products/COGS help takes precedence over marketing copy that still describes the legacy
  constant-cost method.
- The current alert-settings article's daily evaluation and limited history take precedence over
  the broader “real-time” marketing label.
- The old report-feed announcement is evidence of configurable report delivery, not evidence of a
  current general-purpose public CRUD API; verify availability and authentication in-product.
- Current pricing, marketplace coverage, quotas and team limits are deliberately not copied into
  SellerOS configuration.
- sellerboard forecasts, reimbursement values, payouts and some advertising economics are treated
  as estimates where its documentation labels them as such.
